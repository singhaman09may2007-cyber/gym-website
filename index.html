Health & Nutrition Information

Staying fit requires more than just lifting weights. Keep these essential tips in mind:

  • Hydration: Drink at least 3 to 4 liters of water daily to stay hydrated during workouts.
  • Nutrition: Eat a balanced diet rich in proteins, complex carbohydrates, and healthy fats.
  • Rest & Recovery: Allow 7 to 8 hours of sleep for proper muscle repair and growth.
